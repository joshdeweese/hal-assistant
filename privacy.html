Hal privacy policy

Last updated November 2, 2026.

Hal is a personal app used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It does not access anyone else's account.

Google data Hal uses

With the owner's permission, Hal reads, labels and archives messages in the owner's Gmail and creates drafts there.

How the data is handled

  • Sign-in details and processing records are stored on the owner's own computer.
  • Message content is sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic) so it can be sorted and summarized.
  • Summaries and answers are sent to the owner through their own private chat app.

Google data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Removing access

The owner can remove Hal's access at any time in their Google account permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through this site's GitHub page.
